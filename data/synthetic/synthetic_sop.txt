Standard Operating Procedure: Model-X Industrial Air Compressor Startup
Equipment: Model-X
Revision: 2.1
Status: Synthetic Document for Offline Testing

1. Pre-Startup Inspection
Before energizing the compressor, inspect the oil sight glass. The oil level must be within the green operating zone.
Check all emergency stop switches to ensure they are depressed in the disengaged position.
Verify that the air intake filter is clean and free of particulate obstruction.

--- Page 2 ---
2. Energizing Procedure
Turn the main isolator switch on the primary distribution panel clockwise to the ON position.
Observe the digital control module. The system will perform an internal diagnostics check lasting 10 seconds.
Verify that no error codes (ERR-01 through ERR-99) appear on the display screen.
Press and hold the green START button for 2 seconds until the auxiliary cooling fan engages.

--- Page 3 ---
3. Operating Parameters and Pressure Relief
Normal operating pressure is between 6.5 bar and 8.0 bar.
If internal pressure exceeds 9.2 bar, the automatic safety release valve V-12 will actuate.
In the event of an abnormal temperature reading exceeding 95 degrees Celsius, immediately press the Emergency Stop.
Log all hourly pressure readings in the maintenance ledger.
